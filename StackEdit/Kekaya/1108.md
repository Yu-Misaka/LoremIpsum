### Core Proof DAG

#### WZ2 Definition 2.12

> The tubes are essentially distinct. For every $\rho_0 \in [\delta, 1]$, there are $\rho \in [\rho_0, A \rho_0)$ and $\rho$-tubes $\mathcal T_\rho$ such that:
> 1. $\mathcal T_\rho$ is an $A$-uniform partitioning cover of $\mathcal T$
> 2. for each parent, the unit-rescaled child family satisfies the convex Wolff axiom with error $A$.
> 
> 
> This is the root hypothesis; neither a direction-separated cover nor self-similar child counts is stipulated.

Basic Definitions:
1. **$\delta$-tube**: closed $\delta$-neighbourhood of a unit line segment (`Kakeya.Streamlined.TubeFamily`)
2. **Essentially distinct**: $\mathbb T$ is essentially distinct if $T_i \nsubseteq 2 \cdot T_j$ and $T_j \nsubseteq 2 \cdot T_i$ for every $i \neq j$. Here $2 \cdot T$ means dilation about the center of $T$ with factor 2. (`Kakeya.Assouad.wz2PaperCenteredDilatedCarrier`)
3. **$\mathbb T[T_\rho]$**: $\{T \in \mathbb T : T \subseteq T_\rho\}$ (`Kakeya.Assouad.wz2PaperOrdinaryFullFiberIndices`)
4. **Unit rescaling**: 
	- Ellipsoid (centered at $\overrightarrow c$ under linear equivalence $A$): $\overrightarrow c + A(\overline{B(0, 1)})$ (`JohnEllipsoid.ellipsoid`)
	- Outer John ellipsoid of set $K$: (one of) the smallest ellipsoid containing $K$ (`JohnEllipsoid.IsOuterJohnEllipsoid`)
	- Unit rescaling relative to $T_\rho$: choose an outer John ellipsoid of $T_\rho$, suppose it is centered at $\overrightarrow c$ under linear equivalence $A$. Then a unit rescaling relative to $T_\rho$ is affine equivalence $x \mapsto A^{-1}x - c$
